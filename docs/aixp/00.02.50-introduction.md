# Introduction

The arrival of generative AI at the terminal has produced a paradox: teams are producing more syntax than ever, yet shipping durable software feels more fragile, chaotic, and exhausting. 

Navigating this reality requires diagnosing where the industry went wrong, rediscovering why earlier engineering disciplines were built, and understanding the profound economic inversion that occurred when typing ceased to be the constraint.
